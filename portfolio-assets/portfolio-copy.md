## Upwork description
LUNEO is an independent premium e-commerce concept created for the NULLEXWEB portfolio. The fictional handbag brand brings together an editorial visual direction, a cream and burgundy palette, AI-generated campaign imagery and restrained motion.

The implementation includes a responsive Next.js storefront, a filterable and sortable catalogue, product colour selection, a persistent shopping bag and a validated demo checkout. GSAP and ScrollTrigger support considered reveals, while reduced-motion preferences are respected. All 21 concept images are stored locally as optimised WebP assets.

The checkout runs entirely in the browser. No payment is collected, no real order is placed, and no contact details are transmitted. A dedicated OrderService interface prepares the codebase for a future server-side CRM integration.

This is a self-initiated portfolio concept, not commissioned client work. It demonstrates our approach to brand-led commerce design and frontend implementation; no sales uplift or conversion results are claimed.
